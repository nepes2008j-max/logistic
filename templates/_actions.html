They are POST now. The session cookie is SameSite=Lax, so the browser will not attach it to a cross-site POST, and that is what makes the conversion the whole fix rather than half of one. .inline-form is display:contents, so the form box vanishes and the button stays a direct child of whatever row it sits in.
