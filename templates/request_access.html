Membership

A person reads every one of these.

HandShake asks strangers to hand real property to each other, so there is no open sign-up. Tell us who you are and an administrator reviews it by hand. If they approve you, a one-time link lets you set a password.

  1. 01You send this form. We email that address to check it is really yours.
  2. 02Confirm from your inbox, and the request reaches an administrator.
  3. 03If they approve you, a link arrives that works once and lasts seven days.
  4. 04You choose a password on that link, and the account exists from then on.
