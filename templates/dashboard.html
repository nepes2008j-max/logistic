How a hand-off works

  1. Step one

    Ask before you commit

    Chat opens only when both sides agree to it, so your inbox stays yours. Settle the price and where it has to reach in writing.

  2. Step two

    Pay into escrow

    The money leaves your wallet but not the seller's way. HandShake holds it for the whole time the item is travelling.

  3. Step three

    Codes at both doors

    The courier collects against the seller's code and hands over against yours. Reading your code back is what releases the money.
