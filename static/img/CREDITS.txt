Hero truck — inlined in templates/dashboard.html (.flight-truck)

An original delivery box-truck silhouette drawn for this project as one SVG
path. It is not derived from any third-party asset.
  Licence: PUBLIC DOMAIN / CC0. No attribution is required.

A single vector path, inlined into the template rather than linked, so it
inherits the theme's ink through `currentColor` in both light and dark and
costs no extra request.

Why a truck: Global Trade is a logistics marketplace for ordinary goods, and
the goods move by road. The silhouette is a figure for distance covered, not a
description of any fleet.

History, for the next person:
  * The hero once carried a photograph of a Dassault Falcon 7X (registration
    VP-CSW) whose licence this file could not confirm, and which claimed a
    class of service Global Trade does not run. It was withdrawn. The files
    aircraft-1000.* and aircraft-1800.* are what is left of it — referenced by
    nothing and excluded from the repository.
  * It was then replaced by a public-domain airliner silhouette (NIH BioArt
    783-793068, via Wikimedia Commons). That too was withdrawn: an aircraft
    misrepresents a product that delivers by road. The truck replaced it.
