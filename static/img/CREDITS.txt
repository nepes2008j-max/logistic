Processing: the sky was keyed out to alpha locally (per-row sky reference
from the frame margins, blue-minus-red separation, edge-connected flood fill)
so the aircraft could be composited over the hero's own gradient. No part of
the aircraft itself was redrawn or generated.

The aircraft is a visual metaphor for goods crossing distance. HandShake is a
logistics marketplace for ordinary goods; it is not an aviation product.
