A single vector path, inlined into the template rather than linked, so it
inherits the theme's ink through `currentColor` in both light and dark and
costs no extra request. The silhouette is unmodified.

Why this and not the previous asset: the hero used to carry a photograph of a
Dassault Falcon 7X (registration VP-CSW) whose licence this file could not
confirm, and which claimed a class of service HandShake does not run. It was
withdrawn. The files aircraft-1000.* and aircraft-1800.* are what is left of
it — they are referenced by nothing and excluded from the repository.

HandShake is a logistics marketplace for ordinary goods. The aircraft is a
figure for distance covered, not a description of the fleet; the goods move
by road.
